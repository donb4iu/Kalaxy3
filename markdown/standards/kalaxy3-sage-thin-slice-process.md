# Kalaxy3 SAGE End-to-End Thin-Slice Process

## Purpose

The thin slice is the first coherent public introduction to SAGE through one
real Kalaxy3 case. It must explain SAGE's intention and value, preserve human
authority and expertise, show an end-to-end engineering trace, expose measured
outcomes and unknowns, and provide an explicit path for wider participation.

## Canonical case

The first selected case is centralized logging because validated Kalaxy3
evidence spans the full path from intent and a governance pause through staged
implementation, activation, failure recovery, seven-node runtime validation,
availability SLOs, publication, and independent evidence review.

The capability-intelligence walking skeleton remains the governing foundation.
The thin slice does not replace its target model or historical decision cycle.
It presents one applied case using the same federated authority, prediction,
validation, outcome, and evidence principles.

## Required separation

The public case must keep these records distinct:

- source-backed statements;
- SAGE interpretation and recommendation;
- attributed human contribution and authority;
- selected and rejected alternatives;
- measured outcomes;
- unknown or not-yet-measured outcomes.

SAGE remains a decision partner. It may render, compare, and propose, but it
must not claim authority to approve repository, Git, GitHub, or runtime
mutation.

## Validation contract

The repository-owned model, renderer, metrics snapshot, self-test, and
guardrail must prove:

- one operator-selected case and at least one retained or rejected alternative;
- a strictly ordered end-to-end trace;
- evidence references for every source-backed stage and measured outcome;
- explicit human contribution at every stage;
- null actuals for unavailable measurements rather than fabricated zeroes;
- participation paths for readers, operators, experts, engineers, and reviewers;
- a do-nothing alternative;
- no opaque composite score or maturity claim.

## Evidence reconsideration and generational evolution

The public case must make the learning loop understandable without implying that
old evidence dictates current architecture. Initial ideation remains intent-first;
relevant accumulated evidence is then reconsidered for current applicability and
value. If an existing capability materially overlaps a proposed solution, the case
may show reuse, augmentation, replacement, intentional coexistence, or do-nothing
as alternatives, with the Architect retaining the material decision.

The public narrative may also show implementation-generation lineage: what intent
and criteria justified a generation, what later evidence changed, and why a
successor might be retained, adapted, rebuilt, or superseded. Historical choices
remain attributable and evidence-backed. Unmeasured reconsideration efficiency or
modernization value remains explicitly unknown rather than being converted into a
maturity claim.

## Evolution

Additional cases may reuse this contract after their authority, evidence,
alternatives, outcomes, and human contributions are independently validated.
Evidence reuse is not considered learning until later work measures whether
retrieved evidence was applied and whether it reduced rework or time to a
validated implementation.
