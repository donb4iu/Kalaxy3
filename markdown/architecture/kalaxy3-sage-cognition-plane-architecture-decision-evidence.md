## Problem and decision rationale

### Problem or opportunity

The Architect source states that repeated prompt refinement has not reliably kept the persistent Architect-facing LLM focused on long-lived collaboration, evidence-informed innovation, criticism, and intent formulation. The source describes drift into deterministic SAGE control-plane responsibilities and premature implementation mechanics as an architecture problem rather than another prompt-tuning problem.

### Decision

Within Architect-owned authority, establish an explicit three-plane architecture: a persistent Architect interaction plane, isolated fresh/disposable advisory cognition capabilities with typed/provenance-bound handoffs, and a deterministic SAGE governance/execution plane. The role graph is capability-driven rather than a mandatory four-stage waterfall, and SAGE remains the sole deterministic authority for evidence authority/applicability/maturity, governed state, lifecycle/re-entry/recovery, allowed actions, mutation, execution, validation, receipts, and persistence.

This record chronicles that direction; it does not claim the repository has implemented it.

### Decision drivers

- Preserve long-lived objective continuity and engineering value at the Architect interaction surface.
- Isolate probabilistic evidence analysis, innovation, criticism, and intent synthesis from deterministic SAGE control.
- Make role input/output context explicit, typed, provenance-bound, and replayable without inherited conversational memory.
- Preserve historical evidence at actual maturity without requiring implementation qualification for admission.
- Reuse valid role outputs, skip unnecessary capabilities, permit bounded return paths, and surface material Architect choices instead of looping.
- Keep model/provider choice replaceable and subordinate to repository-owned orchestration and SAGE authority.

### Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Advantages | Disadvantages or risks | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continue prompt refinement on one persistent LLM role | Minimal structural change | Architect source reports unreliable role separation and control-plane drift | rejected by Architect direction |
| Mandatory Evidence to Innovator to Critic to Intent waterfall | Simple sequence | Replaces prompt brittleness with workflow brittleness and ignores reusable valid outputs | rejected by Architect constraint |
| Persistent LLM controls cognition choreography | Conversational flexibility | Recreates probabilistic control over workflow state | rejected by Architect constraint |
| New graph/workflow engine now | Explicit graph model | Premature complexity before current repository composition is shown insufficient | deferred by Architect constraint |
| Capability-driven isolated advisory roles under repository-owned deterministic orchestration | Preserves positive LLM value while keeping authority boundaries testable | Requires typed contracts, provenance, and bounded orchestration proof | Architect-directed target |

### Tradeoffs and consequences

- More explicit artifacts and provenance are required between cognition capabilities.
- The persistent collaborator retains positive synthesis and engineering duties but loses implicit workflow-control authority.
- Historical evidence may influence design earlier while remaining visibly historical, partial, uncertain, or revalidation-dependent.
- The bounded MVP must prove usefulness without expanding into broad autonomous multi-agent execution or delaying the shared-inference objective indefinitely.
